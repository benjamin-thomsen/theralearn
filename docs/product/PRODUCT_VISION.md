# TheraLearn – Product Vision

## Purpose

This document owns the detailed permanent product vision for TheraLearn.

Stable project identity and high-level principles belong in `PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md`. Learning mechanisms belong in `LEARNING_MODEL.md`. Durable MVP scope belongs in `mvp.md`. Current project state and next actions belong in `PROJECT_CONTROL.md`.

---

## Product Vision

TheraLearn is intended to provide an integrated learning experience around structured curriculum content.

The platform should connect learning content with multiple ways of working with that same learning context rather than present curriculum, practice, assessment, and progress as unrelated standalone tools.

The currently established product direction includes:

- structured curriculum / syllabus content;
- flashcards;
- quizzes;
- learning progress;
- reading support;
- multilingual support, initially Danish and English.

These capabilities express the product direction. Their detailed learning purpose and relationships are owned by `LEARNING_MODEL.md`, and their inclusion in a specific MVP is owned by `mvp.md`.

---

## Initial Content Domain

Psychotherapy education is the initial content domain for TheraLearn.

This initial focus provides the first concrete learning context for the platform, but it does not define the platform as being permanently limited to psychotherapy education.

TheraLearn's product identity is a structured learning platform; psychotherapy education is its initial domain application.

---

## Integrated Product Principle

The product vision favors one coherent learning environment over a collection of disconnected learning utilities.

Curriculum content provides the learning context around which supported learning activities can be organized. Flashcards, quizzes, progress, reading support, and language support should therefore be understood as parts of the broader learning experience rather than independent product identities.

This principle defines product intent only. It does not prescribe detailed learning mechanics, implementation architecture, or MVP acceptance criteria.

---

## Vision Boundaries

This document does not currently establish:

- detailed target-user personas;
- pricing or business model;
- commercial positioning;
- detailed product success metrics;
- release strategy;
- a prioritized feature roadmap;
- unsupported future feature commitments.

Those subjects require separate verified decisions or evidence before becoming permanent product authority.

---

## Authority Rule

New detailed product-vision claims must be supported by verified decisions or evidence before being added here.

Implementation must not silently redefine Product Vision. Existing implementation can provide evidence about what is currently built, but implementation alone does not create permanent product intent.
